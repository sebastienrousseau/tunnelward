## Highlights ⭐️

* **Gateways as Kubernetes resources**: `tunnelward operator` keeps each `TunnelwardGateway` running as the Helm chart would deploy it, from the same image. Teams declare route ownership with `ConnectorBinding`s and the tool policy with a `RoutePolicy`, next to their own workloads.
* **A broken change never reaches a running gateway**: the operator checks every change with the gateway's own parsers first. A bad policy or a binding with a bad identity leaves the gateway as it is, and the resource's status says why.
* **Tested on a real cluster, with no new dependencies**: `scripts/operator-e2e.sh` creates, changes, breaks and deletes a gateway on k3s. The Kubernetes client is written here over hyper and rustls, where `kube` and `k8s-openapi` would have added 132 packages.
