## Highlights ⭐️

* **The gateway reads MCP**: `tunnelward-mcp` parses each call's JSON-RPC envelope (method, tool, declared client) without its arguments, and refuses a `tools/call` that hides its tool. Observers see every call, and MCP sessions and SSE streams pass through byte for byte.
* **Every request has an id**: a `tw-request-id` the client cannot choose goes to your service and back to the caller, beside the declared User-Agent and W3C `traceparent`.
* **Operate without restarts**: renewed certificates are picked up from disk, and dark mode stops routing to a connector or route without disconnecting it.
* **A connector command and CORS**: `tunnelward connector --config` runs a connector from a TOML file, and per-path CORS rules let browser-based MCP clients in from the origins you allow.
