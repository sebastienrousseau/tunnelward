## Highlights ⭐️

* **Connectors stay connected**: `Connector` keeps two connections to every gateway, from a fixed list or a DNS name it looks up again on an interval, and retries failures with decorrelated-jitter backoff from 250 ms to 30 s.
* **Leases renew without dropping requests**: a connector opens its next connection before the old lease ends, and the gateway retries a request once on another connection when the first refused it unprocessed.
* **Drain with a deadline**: the gateway can drain by connector, route or peer address, letting in-flight requests finish before resetting the rest; `Connector::shutdown` does the same from the connector side and reports whether everything finished.
* **Dead peers are noticed**: HTTP/2 keep-alive pings drop a connector that stops answering, and a CIDR allowlist rejects unexpected connector sources before TLS.
