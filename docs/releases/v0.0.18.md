## Highlights ⭐️

* **Every connector registration is audited**: accepted, refused or failed, each one is a line in the hash-chained log with the identity the certificate proves, the identity the connector claimed, its routes and the error id when refused. `tunnelward audit verify` covers it, and `audit export` writes it as an OCSF Authentication event.
* **Which statement admitted a connector**: an admission by passmcp attestation records the SHA-256 of the statement it used, so an auditor can tie a route to the evidence behind it.
* **No new dependencies**: QUIC, HTTP/3 and the Kubernetes operator wait until their dependencies are vetted.
