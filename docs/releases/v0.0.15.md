## Highlights ⭐️

* **A route can admit only tested MCP servers**: list it under `[attestation]` and a connector must present a passmcp statement about one of the route's targets, signed by a key you trust, recent enough and with the checks you require. cosign bundles work as `cosign attest-blob --statement` writes them; anything else is refused with `TW-R006` or `TW-R007` and the reason.
* **Revoking a statement drains its connections**: add its digest to the revocation file and the gateway drains every connection admitted with it, without touching the others.
* **Renewed statements need no restart**: the connector reads its bundle at every registration, so the next lease carries the statement CI signed last night.
* **Check before you deploy**: `tunnelward attest verify --config gateway.toml --route mcp/payments bundle.json` prints what the statement says and the gateway's decision, offline.
