## Highlights ⭐️

* **Pools and fair load**: every connector that serves a route joins its pool, and of two picked at random the one with fewer requests in flight takes the next request. A connector never holds more than its capacity.
* **Requests wait instead of failing**: with no free slot, a request waits up to `max_wait` (2 s by default) in a bounded queue, then gets `Unavailable` with a `Retry-After`. A route whose connectors left is kept for 2 hours, so a returning connector serves the requests waiting for it.
* **Forwarding to your service**: `tw_connector::Forward` passes each request to a local HTTP/1.1 server over TCP, TLS with a pinned CA, or a Unix socket, answering 502 when it fails and 504 when it is late.
* **Embeddable SDK crates**: the protocol, crypto and connector crates are now MIT OR Apache-2.0, so services can embed them; the gateway and binaries stay GPL-3.0-only, and the gate enforces the boundary.
