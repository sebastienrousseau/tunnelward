## Highlights ⭐️

* **Agents can connect**: the gateway serves HTTP/1.1, and over TLS both HTTP/2 and HTTP/1.1 chosen by ALPN. Responses stream, and a client that stops reading slows the connector through flow control instead of filling the gateway's memory.
* **The real client address**: the socket's peer is what counts. `Forwarded`, `X-Forwarded-For` and PROXY protocol headers are believed only from configured trusted proxies, and the connector receives one clean `Forwarded` header plus the gateway's `Via` hop.
* **Limits at the door**: URLs over 8 KiB, bodies over 16 MiB, slow requests and per-address floods get 414, 413, 504 and 429 with `Retry-After`.
* **Observer hooks**: a `ProxyObserver` sees each request, response, byte count and failure, ready for metrics and audit.
